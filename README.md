# Aura Deck

A skateboard clicker game. Tap the board to ollie, learn tricks, upgrade through 10 boards, ride the skatepark, recruit robot helpers, fight off evil scooters, and Rebirth.

## Play

**https://radicus-boyer.github.io/aura-deck/**

The link picks the right version for your device. To choose by hand, add `?choose` to the end of the link.

- `phone.html` is the phone version: touch controls, with the counter and board pinned at the top.
- `desktop.html` is the PC version: a Fullscreen button (or press **F**) and keyboard controls.

## PC controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Ollie |
| 1-9 | Try your pinned tricks |
| S / L | Shop / Learned moves |
| A | Achievements |
| R | Android Repair |
| M | Sound on/off |
| F | Fullscreen |
| Esc | Close windows |

## Saves

Your progress is saved in your own browser on your own device. Phone and PC don't share saves, and clearing your browser data erases them.
